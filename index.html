Modern users manage many digital accounts. Remembering different complex passwords can be challenging.

SECURITY INSIGHT

Familiar quotations, song lyrics and predictable transformations may be guessable. Adding a few predictable numbers or symbols does not necessarily make a pattern secure.

This demonstration uses predefined examples. Never enter or reuse your real account passwords or private phrases in educational tools.

SECURITY FIRST

Memorability is not enough

A memorable password must also be unique and difficult to guess.

Unique passwords

Use a different password for every account.

Password managers

Use a trusted password manager to generate and store unique passwords.

Multi-factor authentication

Add another layer of authentication wherever possible.

Passkeys

Prefer passkeys where supported for phishing-resistant authentication.
